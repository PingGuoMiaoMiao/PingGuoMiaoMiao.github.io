### 英文翻译
I want to build a [family meal ordering website] (admin side uploads dishes, user side browses and orders, and finally exports a menu image).

Please interview me in depth using the AskUserQuestion tool.

Focus on the tech stack, interaction details, edge cases, potential risks, trade-offs, etc.

Do not ask obvious surface-level questions; dig into the difficult points and blind spots I might have overlooked.

Keep interviewing until we have confirmed all the details, then help me generate a SPEC.md technical specification document.
